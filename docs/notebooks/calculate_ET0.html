Daily equation and assumptions

The implementation uses FAO-56 equation 6:

ET0=0.408Δ(Rn−G)+γ900T+273u2(es−ea)Δ+γ(1+0.34u2). ET_0 = \frac{0.408\Delta(R_n-G) + \gamma\frac{900}{T+273}u_2(e_s-e_a)} {\Delta+\gamma(1+0.34u_2)}.

ET0 is in mm/day; T is daily mean temperature (°C); Rn and G are net radiation and soil heat flux (MJ/m²/day); u2 is wind speed at 2 m (m/s); es and ea are saturation and actual vapour pressure (kPa); Delta and gamma are the slope of the saturation vapour-pressure curve and psychrometric constant (kPa/°C).

  • Daily soil heat flux G is zero; reference albedo is 0.23.
  • Saturation pressure is averaged from daily maximum and minimum temperature. Actual pressure uses mean relative humidity, rather than paired RH extremes.
  • Source wind is assumed to be at 10 m and converted to 2 m. The source measurement height remains unconfirmed.
  • Interior gaps of at most two days in RH and wind are linearly interpolated separately within each calendar year. Longer gaps and gaps at year edges remain missing. Other input variables are not interpolated.
  • Missing inputs propagate to daily ET0, monthly totals and multi-year means. Sums and means do not use na.rm = TRUE.
  • Negative computed ET0 is retained, without clipping to zero.

With e∘(T)=0.6108exp⁡[17.27T/(T+237.3)]e^\circ(T)=0.6108\exp[17.27T/(T+237.3)], mean-RH vapour pressure is ea=(RHmean/100)[e∘(Tmax)+e∘(Tmin)]/2e_a=(RH_{mean}/100)[e^\circ(T_{max})+e^\circ(T_{min})]/2. Wind conversion is u2=uz4.87/ln⁡(67.8z−5.42)u_2=u_z4.87/\ln(67.8z-5.42) with z = 10 m. These are FAO-56 equations 19 and 47. Mean RH is less preferable than paired humidity extremes when available. The available Tmin vapour-pressure fallback is not used to fill RH here.

Net radiation uses absorbed shortwave and FAO-estimated longwave radiation; the solar/clear-sky radiation ratio is capped at 1. Pressure and gamma depend on elevation. Solar geometry retains the FAO denominator 365 even in leap years; monthly sums include every actual date, including 29 February. Polar day/night is outside the implementation’s scope.

Monthly mean weather cannot exactly reproduce daily ET0 because the equation is nonlinear. Changing wind or humidity assumptions requires authorized daily inputs and recalculation, rather than the monthly tables.

Published FAO check

The Uccle example (6 July, FAO-56 example 18) uses the published actual vapour pressure, rather than the NARO mean-RH approximation. The expected result is about 3.9 mm/day. This checks implementation, not the accuracy of every cell.

example_et0 <- pipeline_p0_et0_fao56(
  mean_temperature = matrix(16.9),
  maximum_temperature = matrix(21.5),
  minimum_temperature = matrix(12.3),
  solar_radiation = matrix(22.07),
  actual_vapour_pressure = matrix(1.409),
  wind_2m = matrix(pipeline_p0_wind_at_2m(10 * 1000 / 3600, 10)),
  elevation = 100, latitude = 50.80, day_of_year = 187
)
stopifnot(abs(as.numeric(example_et0) - 3.9) < 0.08)
data.frame(et0_mm_day = as.numeric(example_et0))
  et0_mm_day
1   3.879455

Synthetic daily CSV

The CSV below contains three invented days for one cell. They do not constitute a July total. Matrices have one row per cell and one column per day. Latitude and elevation have one entry per row; day of year has one per column.

daily <- read.csv("data/examples/et0_daily_synthetic.csv")
as_row <- function(x) matrix(x, nrow = 1)
daily$et0_mm_day <- as.numeric(pipeline_p0_et0_fao56(
  mean_temperature = as_row(daily$mean_temperature_c),
  maximum_temperature = as_row(daily$maximum_temperature_c),
  minimum_temperature = as_row(daily$minimum_temperature_c),
  solar_radiation = as_row(daily$solar_radiation_mj_m2_day),
  actual_vapour_pressure = as_row(pipeline_p0_actual_vapour_pressure_rh(
    daily$relative_humidity_percent, daily$maximum_temperature_c,
    daily$minimum_temperature_c)),
  wind_2m = as_row(pipeline_p0_wind_at_2m(daily$wind_10m_m_s, 10)),
  elevation = daily$elevation_m[1], latitude = daily$latitude_degrees[1],
  day_of_year = as.integer(format(as.Date(daily$date), "%j"))
))
daily[c("date", "et0_mm_day")]

For gaps, call pipeline_p0_fill_short_gaps() separately on each year’s RH and wind before the equation, as in the local bundle’s export_yearly.R. The low-level pipeline_p0_monthly_sums() does not check calendar completeness: supply every date exactly once, in matching order, for a complete month (28/29/30/31 days) before reporting a monthly total.

Local monthly products

Year-specific files contain 12 monthly totals per mesh/year, covering January 2008 through December 2018 (132 months per variable per cell). The means table contains 12 mean monthly totals: July is the mean of the eleven July totals. These are 11-year averages, not standard 30-year climate normals.

The prepared bundle covers 377,449 meshes, 153 primary-mesh blocks and 4,151,939 mesh-year rows. Its verification.csv records agreement of yearly averages with the supplied means within 1e-8 mm (maximum difference 4.77484718430787e-12 mm). This verifies aggregation, not meteorological accuracy. SHA256SUMS records bundle integrity.

Set PROJECT_DATA_DIR in the ignored .Renviron and restart R after editing. The following example is deliberately not executed during rendering:

bundle <- et0_data_dir() # PROJECT_DATA_DIR/naro-et0/2008-2018
normal <- read_monthly_csv(file.path(bundle, "data",
  "monthly_normals_2008_2018.csv.gz"))
yearly_files <- list.files(file.path(bundle, "data", "yearly"),
  pattern = "^monthly_[0-9]{4}_2008_2018[.]csv[.]gz$", full.names = TRUE)
# One block, not all Japan:
one_block <- read_monthly_csv(yearly_files[1])
period_summary(one_block, 7:9, "Jul-Sep")
winter_summary(one_block)

Base R reads gzip CSVs directly. read_monthly_csv() keeps mesh3_code as character. et0_data_dir() fails if the environment variable is unset. Monthly columns are et0_fao56_Jan through et0_fao56_Dec and precipitation_Jan through precipitation_Dec, in mm/month. See the bundle’s data-dictionary.csv and STATUS.md. Annual totals in the means table sum its 12 months. et0_filled_days is the maximum of separate RH and wind filled-day counts per cell/year, summed across 11 years in the means table. It is not their union or a count of missing days in all input variables.

Month windows and AI

Sum monthly totals; do not multiply them by days again. ET0 and precipitation become depths in mm per selected period. AI is sum(P)/sum(ET0) over identical months, rather than the mean of monthly AI. Missing months propagate and non-positive or missing ET0 denominators give NA. P−ET0 is a climatic water balance, not a soil-water balance model.

Winter crosses years

Winter Y combines December Y−1 with January-February Y. period_summary(data, c(12, 1, 2)) only selects months within the same row. Use winter_summary() on year-specific rows for a chronological winter. It aligns by mesh and year, preserves input rows, and labels winter by the year of January-February. Missing prior December yields NA.

## Daily equation and assumptions

The implementation uses [FAO-56 equation 6](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e06.htm):

$$
ET_0 = \frac{0.408\Delta(R_n-G)
 + \gamma\frac{900}{T+273}u_2(e_s-e_a)}
 {\Delta+\gamma(1+0.34u_2)}.
$$

ET0 is in mm/day; T is daily mean temperature (°C); Rn and G are net radiation
and soil heat flux (MJ/m²/day); u2 is wind speed at 2 m (m/s); es and ea are
saturation and actual vapour pressure (kPa); Delta and gamma are the slope of
the saturation vapour-pressure curve and psychrometric constant (kPa/°C).

- Daily soil heat flux G is zero; reference albedo is 0.23.
- Saturation pressure is averaged from daily maximum and minimum temperature.
  Actual pressure uses mean relative humidity, rather than paired RH extremes.
- Source wind is **assumed** to be at 10 m and converted to 2 m. The source
  measurement height remains unconfirmed.
- Interior gaps of at most two days in RH and wind are linearly interpolated
  separately within each calendar year. Longer gaps and gaps at year edges
  remain missing. Other input variables are not interpolated.
- Missing inputs propagate to daily ET0, monthly totals and multi-year means.
  Sums and means do not use `na.rm = TRUE`.
- Negative computed ET0 is retained, without clipping to zero.

With $e^\circ(T)=0.6108\exp[17.27T/(T+237.3)]$, mean-RH vapour pressure is
$e_a=(RH_{mean}/100)[e^\circ(T_{max})+e^\circ(T_{min})]/2$.
Wind conversion is $u_2=u_z4.87/\ln(67.8z-5.42)$ with z = 10 m.
These are [FAO-56 equations 19 and 47](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e07.htm).
Mean RH is less preferable than paired humidity extremes when available.
The available Tmin vapour-pressure fallback is **not** used to fill RH here.

Net radiation uses absorbed shortwave and FAO-estimated longwave radiation;
the solar/clear-sky radiation ratio is capped at 1. Pressure and gamma depend
on elevation. Solar geometry retains the FAO denominator 365 even in leap
years; monthly sums include every actual date, including 29 February.
Polar day/night is outside the implementation's scope.

Monthly mean weather cannot exactly reproduce daily ET0 because the equation
is nonlinear. Changing wind or humidity assumptions requires authorized daily
inputs and recalculation, rather than the monthly tables.

## 日別計算式と仮定

[FAO-56の式6](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e06.htm)を使用します。

$$
ET_0 = \frac{0.408\Delta(R_n-G)
 + \gamma\frac{900}{T+273}u_2(e_s-e_a)}
 {\Delta+\gamma(1+0.34u_2)}.
$$

ET₀はmm/日、Tは日平均気温（℃）、RnとGは純放射・地中熱流量（MJ/m²/日）、
u2は地上2 mの風速（m/s）、esとeaは飽和・実水蒸気圧（kPa）です。
Deltaは飽和水蒸気圧曲線の傾き、gammaは乾湿計定数（いずれもkPa/℃）です。

- 日別の地中熱流量Gは0、基準面のアルベドは0.23とします。
- 飽和水蒸気圧は日最高・最低気温から求めた値の平均です。実水蒸気圧には、
  最高・最低相対湿度の組ではなく日平均相対湿度を用います。
- 元の風速は**地上10 mの値と仮定**し、2 mへ換算します。NAROの元データが表す高度は未確認です。
- 相対湿度と風速について、それぞれ各暦年内で、観測値に挟まれた最大2日間の欠測を線形補間します。
  より長い欠測と年の端の欠測は残します。他の入力変数は補間しません。
- 未補間の欠測は日別ET₀、月合計、多年平均に伝播します。合計・平均に`na.rm = TRUE`は使いません。
- 計算された負のET₀は0に切り上げず、そのまま保持します。

$e^\circ(T)=0.6108\exp[17.27T/(T+237.3)]$として、日平均相対湿度による近似は
$e_a=(RH_{mean}/100)[e^\circ(T_{max})+e^\circ(T_{min})]/2$です。
風速換算は$u_2=u_z4.87/\ln(67.8z-5.42)$、z = 10 mとします。
[FAO-56の式19・47](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e07.htm)に対応します。
相対湿度の極値が得られる場合は、その組を使う方法が望まれます。
実装にある最低気温による代替関数は、今回の相対湿度欠測の補完には**使いません**。

純放射は吸収短波放射とFAO式による長波放射から計算し、日射量／晴天日射量の比の上限は1です。
気圧と乾湿計定数は標高から計算します。太陽位置の式の分母365は閏年も維持しますが、
月合計には2月29日を含む実際の全日付を含めます。白夜・極夜は実装の対象外です。

式は非線形なので、月平均の気象値から日別計算を正確に再現することはできません。
風速・湿度の仮定を変更するには、利用許可のある日別入力から再計算する必要があります。

```{r}
    daily$relative_humidity_percent, daily$maximum_temperature_c,
    daily$minimum_temperature_c)),
  wind_2m = as_row(pipeline_p0_wind_at_2m(daily$wind_10m_m_s, 10)),
  elevation = daily$elevation_m[1], latitude = daily$latitude_degrees[1],
  day_of_year = as.integer(format(as.Date(daily$date), "%j"))
))
daily[c("date", "et0_mm_day")]
```

::::: {.content-visible unless-profile="ja"}

For gaps, call `pipeline_p0_fill_short_gaps()` separately on each year's RH
and wind before the equation, as in the local bundle's `export_yearly.R`.
The low-level `pipeline_p0_monthly_sums()` does not check calendar completeness:
supply every date exactly once, in matching order, for a complete month
(28/29/30/31 days) before reporting a monthly total.

## Local monthly products

Year-specific files contain 12 monthly totals per mesh/year, covering January
2008 through December 2018 (132 months per variable per cell). The means table
contains 12 mean monthly totals: July is the mean of the eleven July totals.
These are 11-year averages, **not standard 30-year climate normals**.

The prepared bundle covers 377,449 meshes, 153 primary-mesh blocks and
4,151,939 mesh-year rows. Its `verification.csv` records agreement of yearly
averages with the supplied means within 1e-8 mm (maximum difference
4.77484718430787e-12 mm). This verifies aggregation, not meteorological accuracy.
`SHA256SUMS` records bundle integrity.

Set `PROJECT_DATA_DIR` in the ignored `.Renviron` and restart R after editing.
The following example is deliberately **not executed during rendering**:

:::::

::::: {.content-visible when-profile="ja"}

欠測がある場合は、ローカル一式の`export_yearly.R`と同様に、各年の相対湿度・風速へ
それぞれ`pipeline_p0_fill_short_gaps()`を適用してから計算します。
低水準関数`pipeline_p0_monthly_sums()`は暦の完全性を検査しません。
月合計を報告する前に、28・29・30・31日という月の長さに応じて全日付を過不足なく、
入力間で同じ順に揃えてください。

## ローカルの月別データ

年別ファイルはメッシュ・年ごとに12か月の合計を持ち、2008年1月〜2018年12月を網羅します。
1メッシュ・1変数あたり132か月です。平均表には11年分の各月合計の平均を格納します。
例えば7月の値は11個の7月合計の平均であり、**標準的な30年平年値ではありません**。

用意済みのデータは377,449メッシュ・153一次メッシュブロック・4,151,939メッシュ年行です。
`verification.csv`には、年別表から再計算した平均と既存平均表との差が1e-8 mm未満
（最大4.77484718430787e-12 mm）であることが記録されています。
これは集計の整合性の確認であり、気象値の正確さを保証するものではありません。
`SHA256SUMS`はファイルの完全性を確認するための一覧です。

Git管理外の`.Renviron`に`PROJECT_DATA_DIR`を設定し、変更後はRを再起動します。
以下の実データ読込例は、**描画時には実行しません**。

:::::

```{r}
#| label: et0-local-access
#| eval: false
bundle <- et0_data_dir() # PROJECT_DATA_DIR/naro-et0/2008-2018
normal <- read_monthly_csv(file.path(bundle, "data",
  "monthly_normals_2008_2018.csv.gz"))
```

::::: {.content-visible unless-profile="ja"}

Base R reads gzip CSVs directly. `read_monthly_csv()` keeps `mesh3_code` as
character. `et0_data_dir()` fails if the environment variable is unset.
Monthly columns are `et0_fao56_Jan` through `et0_fao56_Dec` and
`precipitation_Jan` through `precipitation_Dec`, in mm/month. See the bundle's
`data-dictionary.csv` and `STATUS.md`. Annual totals in the means table sum
its 12 months. `et0_filled_days` is the maximum of separate RH and wind
filled-day counts per cell/year, summed across 11 years in the means table.
It is not their union or a count of missing days in all input variables.

## Month windows and AI

Sum monthly totals; do not multiply them by days again. ET0 and precipitation
become depths in mm per selected period. AI is **sum(P)/sum(ET0)** over identical
months, rather than the mean of monthly AI. Missing months propagate and
non-positive or missing ET0 denominators give `NA`. P−ET0 is a climatic water
balance, not a soil-water balance model.

The synthetic CSV below has two invented identifiers and three years of
illustrative monthly totals, independent of the daily example above.

:::::

::::: {.content-visible when-profile="ja"}

base Rでgzip圧縮CSVを直接読めます。`read_monthly_csv()`は`mesh3_code`を文字列として保持します。
環境変数が未設定の場合、`et0_data_dir()`は停止します。
月別列は`et0_fao56_Jan`〜`et0_fao56_Dec`と`precipitation_Jan`〜`precipitation_Dec`で、
単位はmm/月です。ローカル一式の`data-dictionary.csv`と`STATUS.md`も参照してください。
平均表の年合計は12か月の和です。`et0_filled_days`は、各メッシュ・年の湿度と風速の
補間日数の大きい方で、平均表では11年分を合計します。両変数の補間日の和集合や、
すべての入力変数における欠測日数ではありません。

## 月の窓と乾燥指数AI

月合計を足し合わせます。日数を再度掛けてはいけません。ET₀・降水量は選択期間あたりのmmです。
AIは同じ月の窓について**sum(P)/sum(ET0)**とし、月ごとのAIの算術平均にはしません。
欠測月は集計値に伝播し、ET₀の分母が0以下または欠測ならAIは`NA`です。
P−ET₀は気候学的な水収支であり、土壌水分モデルではありません。

次の架空CSVは、実在メッシュを表さない2個の識別子と3年分の月合計を持ちます。
上の日別CSVとは独立した説明用の数値です。

:::::

```{r}
#| label: et0-synthetic-monthly
yearly <- read_monthly_csv("data/examples/et0_monthly_synthetic.csv")
jas <- period_summary(yearly, 7:9, "Jul-Sep")
jas
columns <- c(paste0("et0_fao56_", month.abb),
  paste0("precipitation_", month.abb))
normal <- yearly[yearly$year == 2008, "mesh3_code", drop = FALSE]
for (column in columns) {
  normal[[column]] <- vapply(normal$mesh3_code, function(key) {
    mean(yearly[yearly$mesh3_code == key, column]) # no na.rm
  }, numeric(1))
}
climate_jas <- period_summary(normal, 7:9, "Jul-Sep")
data.frame(mesh3_code = normal$mesh3_code,
  ratio_of_climatological_sums = climate_jas$aridity_index,
  mean_yearly_ai = vapply(normal$mesh3_code, function(key) {
    mean(jas$aridity_index[jas$mesh3_code == key])
  }, numeric(1)))
```

::::: {.content-visible unless-profile="ja"}

A ratio of climatological sums (the original production definition) generally
differs from the mean yearly AI. Averaging monthly AI also changes the weighting:

:::::

::::: {.content-visible when-profile="ja"}

平年合計の比（元の計算で使うAIの定義）は、年別AIの平均とは一般に一致しません。
月別AIを平均した場合も、次の例のように重み付けが変わります。

:::::

```{r}
#| label: et0-ai-weighting
p <- c(50, 200)
et0 <- c(100, 50)
data.frame(ratio_of_sums = sum(p) / sum(et0), mean_monthly_ai = mean(p / et0))
```

::::: {.content-visible unless-profile="ja"}

### Winter crosses years

Winter Y combines December Y−1 with January-February Y.
`period_summary(data, c(12, 1, 2))` only selects months within the same row.
Use `winter_summary()` on year-specific rows for a chronological winter.
It aligns by mesh and year, preserves input rows, and labels winter by the
year of January-February. Missing prior December yields `NA`.

:::::

::::: {.content-visible when-profile="ja"}

### 年をまたぐ冬季

冬季Yは前年Y−1の12月と当年Yの1〜2月を結合します。
`period_summary(data, c(12, 1, 2))`だけでは同じ行の月を選ぶため、この時系列の冬季にはなりません。
年別行へ`winter_summary()`を使うと、メッシュ・年で対応付け、入力行を保持し、
1〜2月の年で冬季を表します。前年12月がなければ`NA`を返します。

:::::

```{r}
#| label: et0-synthetic-winter
winter_summary(yearly)
```

::::: {.content-visible unless-profile="ja"}

For the complete real export, winters ending in 2009–2018 are available.
Winter 2008 lacks December 2007; winter 2019 lacks January-February 2019 and
has no anchor row. Summing December, January and February **means** describes
an average seasonal window, not a specific winter or its variability.

## Reproduction and verification

`R/pipeline_p0_et0.R` is unchanged from the standalone bundle, originating at
`japanese-trees-sdms` commit `1731a9e9fcaba87d718168e5eed0aeaed68b2acd`.
The gap helper is extracted unchanged from its `R/pipeline_p0_naro.R`.
`R/et0_support.R` adds CSV reading, paths and seasonal summaries. No new package
dependency is required. The export environment was R 4.5.3 and terra 1.8.86,
as recorded in the bundle's `build-environment.csv`. Optional regeneration
from authorized NetCDFs uses terra and the local bundle scripts. Raw inputs
remain immutable. This integration does not retrain an SDM.

Download the [calculation functions](../R/pipeline_p0_et0.R),
[support functions](../R/et0_support.R), [synthetic daily CSV](../data/examples/et0_daily_synthetic.csv)
and [synthetic monthly CSV](../data/examples/et0_monthly_synthetic.csv).

```bash
Rscript --vanilla tests/test_et0.R
Rscript tests/verify_et0_local.R
quarto render notebooks/calculate_ET0.qmd --profile en
quarto render notebooks/calculate_ET0.qmd --profile ja
```

The first script uses base R and synthetic inputs. The second reads all local
yearly tables via `PROJECT_DATA_DIR`, validates keys and coverage, and compares
their means with the supplied table. It prints aggregate statistics only.
Rendering this chapter never reads NARO tables.
